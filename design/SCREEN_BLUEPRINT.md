# Screen Blueprint

## Global navigation
Keep navigation compact. Do not show shop, gems, ranking, or excessive account controls in MVP.

Recommended persistent actions:
- Map
- Mission progress
- Settings/help
- Camera privacy status where relevant

## 1. Landing
Composition:
- Paper/grid background
- Logo top-left
- Large headline and CTA
- Hero mascot overlapping the composition
- Projectile doodle, formula marks, target, and hand-gesture cue

Primary CTA: เริ่มการเดินทาง
Secondary CTA: ทดลองด่วน

## 2. World Map
Composition:
- Wide ocean scene
- Five or six islands
- Projectile Island highlighted
- Locked islands desaturated
- Dotted route and small boat/compass detail
- Minimal utility controls on the right edge

Clicking Projectile Island opens its hub. Locked islands open only a short preview modal.

## 3. Projectile Island Hub
Composition:
- Island landscape in the upper area
- Mascot guide with short speech bubble
- Mission path/cards along the lower area

Playable: Basic Shot
Preview: Angle Master, Wall Challenge, Boss

## 4. Mission Brief
Composition:
- Paper mission card centered
- Gesture illustrations in three steps
- Short learning objective
- One primary CTA

Avoid long paragraphs.

## 5. Prediction
Composition:
- Paper panel
- One large question
- Three or four image-led answer choices
- Thinking mascot sticker

No timer in MVP.

## 6. Gameplay
Composition:
- Camera feed or neutral fallback background fills most of screen
- Simulation objects layered clearly
- Hand outline/landmarks visible when enabled
- Angle and strength HUD in one corner
- Reset and fallback control
- Small status text: detecting, grabbed, aiming, released

Do not place large mascot art over the play field.

## 7. Compare
Composition:
- Graph-paper background
- Two attempt cards or combined trajectory graph
- Numeric values below each attempt
- One observation prompt

Prioritize comparison over decoration.

## 8. Concept Check
Composition:
- Lab notebook page
- One question requiring reasoning
- Short answer or selected response
- Scientist mascot sticker as guide

## 9. Summary
Composition:
- Open lab-report notebook
- Learning evidence on left
- Strengths and next step on right
- Badge polaroid/sticker
- Replay and return-to-map actions

Do not reduce the summary to stars only.
