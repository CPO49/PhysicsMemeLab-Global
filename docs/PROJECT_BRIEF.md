# Project Brief — Meme Physics Archipelago

## Product summary
Meme Physics Archipelago is a browser-based physics learning game in which learners travel through themed islands, use camera gestures to control simulations, and complete a learning loop of prediction, experimentation, comparison, explanation, and assessment.

The product addresses a common learning problem: some learners can remember formulas and substitute values but still struggle to connect variables with real physical situations.

## Track 3 alignment
The product follows this chain:

1. Learner signals and evidence reveal difficulty connecting formulas to physical situations.
2. The team converts that insight into a concrete learning product.
3. The product collects behavior during experimentation.
4. The system measures understanding before and after the mission.

Memes are used as a familiar communication language, interaction theme, and mission context. Gesture, simulation, comparison, and reflection are the mechanisms that create learning.

## Approved product identity
- Working name: **Meme Physics Archipelago**
- Presentation name may remain **Meme Physics Lab** if already used in code.
- World structure: an archipelago of physics islands.
- Each island represents one physics topic and a recognizable meme-driven situation.
- Progress is unlocked through demonstrated understanding, not only game completion.

## Island structure
### Playable in MVP
- Projectile Island — projectile motion

### Preview or locked only
- Momentum Island — momentum and impulse
- Balance 67 Island — torque and equilibrium
- Friction Island — friction and stopping
- Energy Island — potential and kinetic energy
- Mystery Island — future expansion

## Primary user
Upper-secondary learners studying introductory physics, starting with projectile motion.

## Core learning outcome
After the mission, the learner should be able to explain how launch angle and initial speed affect trajectory, maximum height, and horizontal range in the simulated situation.

## Core experience
Predict → Perform gesture → Observe trajectory → Compare attempts → Explain → Receive learning report

## MVP guardrails
- One playable island
- One complete projectile mission
- Other islands are non-playable previews
- No required login
- No production database
- No shop, real currency, ranking, or multiplayer
- Camera is primary, mouse/touch fallback is mandatory
- Use original or appropriately licensed mascots and assets; respect third-party intellectual property
